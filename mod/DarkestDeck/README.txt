Darkest Deck - Darkest Dungeon heroes as Balatro Jokers, with Stress.

Needs Balatro (with lovely-injector) and your own installed copy of Darkest Dungeon:
hero stats and portraits are read from it at runtime; none of its files are included.
Installed and launched by Melty (melty.gg), which tells the mod where Darkest Dungeon
is by writing DD_PATH into companion.cfg before every Play.
Log: %AppData%/Balatro/darkestdeck.log
